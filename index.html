PVS Screen Recorder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026
App: PVS Screen Recorder (Android package com.pvs.recorder)
Developer: PVS Khalsa

PVS Screen Recorder records your phone's screen, with media sound and, if you choose, your microphone, and saves the video on your phone. This page explains what the app does with information.

Summary

  • The developer does not collect, receive or store any data from the app.
  • The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking code.
  • Your recordings and settings stay on your phone. Android automatic backup is disabled; device-maker transfer tools may behave differently.

What the app records

While a recording is running, the app captures what is shown on your screen. Depending on the sound setting you choose, it also records the phone's media sound ("Media audio only") or the media sound mixed with your microphone ("Media audio + microphone"). The result is saved as a video file in the folder Movies/PVS Screen Recorder on your phone.

Android itself asks for your permission every time a recording starts, and Android shows its own recording indicator while the capture runs. A recording only starts after you agree in that Android dialog. You can pause, resume or stop the recording from the app or from its notification at any time.

Recordings never leave your phone by themselves. The Recordings tab lets you play, share, rename or delete each file. Sharing uses the app you pick, and what that app does with the video is covered by that app's own privacy policy. Deleting a recording removes it from your phone.

What stays on your phone

PVS Screen Recorder saves your choices on your phone: the sound source, the microphone you picked, the resolution, the video bitrate, and what to do when the screen turns off. The app opts out of Android's automatic backup (allowBackup is false in its manifest), so it does not ask Android to back this data up. Removing the app deletes these settings. The Android setting "Clear storage" deletes them too. Video files in the Movies folder are normal media files: removing the app does not delete them, and this page does not cover copies a phone maker's own transfer tool may make.

Permissions

The app declares exactly these permissions: POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION and RECORD_AUDIO. It does not declare INTERNET, location, contacts, call log, SMS or camera permissions.

  • Notifications: shows the recording notice with Stop and Pause or Resume buttons, so you can control the recording from any app. You can refuse it; recording controls then only work inside the app.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): used only while a recording runs and only if you chose "Media audio + microphone". The microphone sound goes into the video file on your phone and nowhere else.
  • Foreground service: keeps the recording running while the app is closed, as Android requires for screen capture.

The microphone choice and every other setting are off or at their defaults until you change them, and the app never starts recording without your tap plus Android's consent dialog.

Screen capture access

To record the screen, the app uses Android's MediaProjection service. Android grants this access one recording at a time through its own consent dialog, and the access ends when the recording stops. The app does not read your screen at any other time.

Update checks

The app contains no update-check code. If you installed it from Google Play, the Google Play Store app may update it as part of its normal operation; that work is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
